## Active Listening

### Question 1: Write a short situation where you demonstrate Active Listening.

**Answer:**

1. Give your full attention.
2. Maintain eye contact.
3. Use positive body language.
4. Do not interrupt.
5. Show that you are interested.
6. Ask questions for clarification.

### Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

**Answer:**

1. Focus completely on the conversation and avoid distractions.
2. Try to understand the speaker's point of view without judging them.
3. Show empathy and make the speaker feel comfortable sharing their thoughts.
4. Pay attention to the speaker's emotions and mood.
5. Reflect the speaker's feelings through your words and body language.
6. Repeat or explain the speaker's main idea in your own words.
7. Check with the speaker whether you understood their message correctly.
8. Listen to understand the speaker, not just to prepare your own response.

## Reflection

#### Question 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?

**Answer:**

1. I get distracted by my phone, notifications, or other things around me.
2. Sometimes I think about other things while someone is speaking.
3. I may interrupt the speaker before they finish talking.
4. Sometimes I make assumptions before fully understanding the message.
5. I may lose concentration when the conversation is long or complicated.
6. Sometimes my emotions affect the way I listen.
7. I may focus on what I want to say instead of listening to the speaker.





## Types of Communication

 - Passive Communication

 - Aggressive Communication

 - Passive-Aggressive Communication

 - Assertive Communication



### Question 1: When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?

**Answer:**

1. I become passive when I am afraid of hurting someone's feelings.
2. Sometimes I stay quiet even when I disagree with someone.
3. I become passive when I want to avoid an argument or conflict.
4. Sometimes I say "yes" even when I do not want to do something.
5. I may avoid sharing my opinion when I am talking to someone senior or more experienced.
6. Sometimes I keep my problems to myself instead of expressing them clearly.

### Question 2: When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

**Answer:**

1. I may become aggressive when I am very angry or frustrated.
2. Sometimes I raise my voice when I strongly disagree with someone.
3. I may interrupt someone when I feel that they are not listening to me.
4. Sometimes I become aggressive when someone repeatedly makes the same mistake.
5. I may blame someone instead of calmly explaining the problem.
6. Sometimes I try to force my opinion when I strongly believe that I am right.

### Question 3: When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

**Answer:**

1. Sometimes I use sarcasm when I am angry but do not want to say it directly.
2. I may give taunts when someone's actions have upset me.
3. Sometimes I stop talking to someone instead of telling them what is bothering me.
4. I may complain about a person to someone else instead of discussing the problem with that person.
5. Sometimes I intentionally delay doing something when I am unhappy about it.
6. I may say "it's fine" even when I am actually upset with someone.

### Question 4: How can you make your communication assertive?

**Answer:**

1. I will clearly express my thoughts, feelings, and needs instead of keeping them to myself.
2. I will use simple and respectful words instead of blaming or attacking others.
3. I will use "I" statements, such as "I feel..." or "I think..." instead of saying "You always..." or "You never...".
4. I will listen to the other person's opinion and respect their point of view.
5. I will learn to say "no" politely when I cannot or do not want to do something.
6. I will stay calm and control my emotions during difficult conversations.
7. I will maintain proper eye contact and use confident but respectful body language.
8. I will focus on solving the problem instead of trying to prove that I am right.
9. I will practice assertive communication in small, everyday situations so that it becomes a habit.
10. I will try to find a solution that respects both my needs and the other person's needs.
