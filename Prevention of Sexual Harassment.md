## What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment can include any unwanted sexual behaviour that makes a person feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or disrespected. Some examples are:

* Making sexual comments, jokes, or inappropriate remarks.
* Sending or showing sexual messages, images, videos etc.
* Making comments about someone's body, appearance.
* Repeatedly asking for dates or sexual relationships after the person has shown no interest.
* Making sexual gestures, staring, or behaving in a sexually inappropriate manner.
* Asking for sexual favours in return for workplace benefits.
* Making threats or creating pressure because someone rejected unwanted sexual behaviour.
* Repeated unwanted behaviour that creates a hostile, uncomfortable.


## What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face or witness such behaviour, I would take it seriously and not ignore it. If I feel safe, I would clearly communicate that the behaviour is inappropriate and should stop.

I would also:

* Note down the date, time, location, people involved, and what happened.
* Keep any relevant messages, emails, screenshots.
* Report the incident to the appropriate manager, HR, or the designated POSH
* If I witness harassment, I would support the affected person and encourage them to report it.
* I would maintain confidentiality and avoid spreading rumours or discussing the incident unnecessarily.
* If the behaviour is repeated, I would report it even if the person has already been asked to stop.
* I would cooperate with the investigation and provide truthful information if required.

## How to handle cases of harassment?

If someone faces or witnesses harassment, they should take the situation seriously and should not ignore it. If it is safe to do so, they can clearly communicate that the behaviour is inappropriate and should stop. They should also keep a record of the incident and report it through the appropriate workplace channel, such as a manager, HR, or the organisation's harassment/PoSH committee. Witnesses should also support the affected person and cooperate if an investigation takes place.

## How to behave appropriately?

Everyone should behave professionally and respectfully in the workplace. We should respect other people's personal boundaries and avoid unwanted physical contact, sexual comments, inappropriate jokes, gestures, messages, or requests. Before making personal or physical interactions, we should consider whether the other person is comfortable with them. If someone indicates that a behaviour is unwanted, we should stop it immediately and respect their decision.
