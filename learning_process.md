
## How to Learn Faster with the Feynman Technique

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line

Ans: The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you deeply understand a concept by explaining it in simple, jargon-free language

## Learning How to Learn TED talk by Barbara Oakley

### Question 2: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

Ans: The most interesting idea for me was the difference between focused mode and diffuse mode. I found it interesting that taking a break from a difficult problem can help our brain make new connections and find solutions. The examples of Thomas Edison and Salvador Dalí showed me that relaxing the mind can also help us learn and come up with new ideas.

### Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

Ans: Focused mode is when we concentrate our attention on a particular problem or task and use our familiar ways of thinking to solve it. Diffuse mode is a more relaxed state of thinking where our mind can make connections between different ideas. According to the video, we need both modes for effective learning and problem-solving.


## Learn Anything in 20 hours

### Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.

Ans: According to Josh Kaufman’s “The First 20 Hours,How to Learn Anything” video, the steps for approaching a new topic are:
     - Deconstruct the skill.
     - Learn enough to self-correct.
     - Remove practice barriers.
     - Practice for at least 20 hours.


## Question 5: What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?
Ans: Going forward, I will take the following actions to improve my learning process:
    - Give 100% attention when learning and try to understand the topic deeply.
    - Explain the concepts in my own words.
    - Understand concepts clearly and practice them with my own code.
    - Tell others early when I face any problem or difficulty.
    - Focus on learning a few skills very well.
    - Track my time to use it more effectively.
    - Avoid unnecessary things and distractions while learning.
